# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
Readers of the personal portfolio and technical blog; potential professional collaborators assessing Adrián's AI, data science and ML engineering work.

## Product Purpose
Present professional experience alongside working projects and writing. This redesign explores five options before choosing an implementation. Travel Ruter is the primary project, as requested.

## Brand Personality
Content-first, technical, personal. Preserve the existing restrained visual identity while exploring different compositions.

## Anti-references
The current hierarchy does not satisfy the owner. Avoid an exhaustive CV as the entire homepage, decorative gradients that weaken legibility, and generic repeated skill cards.

## Design Principles
- Show Travel Ruter as evidence of end-to-end product ownership.
- Ground profile claims in the three supplied CVs.
- Keep writing and experience accessible from the homepage.
- Explore distinct compositions within the existing visual language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Use legible contrast, semantic navigation, keyboard-accessible controls, responsive layouts and reduced-motion support as implementation defaults.
